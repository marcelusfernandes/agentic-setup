`ci/issue-lint.mts` refuses to dispatch two open issues of one milestone whose `## Files` globs
overlap with no order between them, and M18's issues were cut one defect at a time over files
they share, so the milestone reached a state in which almost nothing could be claimed: the
closing comment on #291 records a dispatch plan that ended in sixteen rounds of one issue each,
not because the work was serial but because the queue was. Collapsing thirty-one issues into
eleven sweeps is what made the remainder dispatchable, and the twenty-one issues moved to M19
carry the same reason in their move notes — queued behind an open sibling, with nothing wrong
or unwanted about the issue itself. This file is a dated snapshot at the SHA above, not a claim
that the sweeps exhausted what they absorbed.

repaired. `docs/closeout/README.md` step 2 has the phase's decision-log lines copied verbatim

`scripts/log-decision.mts` appends to a single comment marked `<!-- agentic-decision-log -->`
on the milestone's **parent** issue, and M18 has no parent — it was drawn as a cut, and no
issue of the milestone is a spec issue. Measured on 2026-09-20: each of the five earlier
phases' parents (#142, #153, #160, #175 and #201) carries exactly one comment with that marker,
